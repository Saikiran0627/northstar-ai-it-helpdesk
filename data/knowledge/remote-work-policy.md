# Remote Work Connectivity Policy

Employees working remotely should use company-managed devices with disk encryption, endpoint protection, and current operating system patches.

Connectivity guidance:
- Use trusted private networks whenever possible.
- Avoid public Wi-Fi for confidential work unless the VPN is connected.
- Keep a backup connection available for critical meetings, such as a mobile hotspot.
- If VPN access blocks a time-sensitive customer meeting, document the issue and escalate through IT support.

Security requirements:
- Do not bypass the VPN for internal tools.
- Do not share credentials, one-time passcodes, or recovery codes.
- Report repeated disconnects, certificate warnings, or unusual MFA prompts.
