# Ticket Escalation Policy

The AI IT Help Desk may draft support tickets but must receive explicit employee confirmation before creating or modifying a ticket.

Priority guidelines:
- High priority: customer-facing meetings blocked within the next hour, repeated VPN disconnects, suspected account compromise, or broad service degradation.
- Normal priority: individual software errors, access requests, hardware questions, or non-urgent troubleshooting.
- Low priority: how-to questions and planned equipment requests.

Ticket fields:
- Short title describing the affected service.
- Employee-provided description and business impact.
- Priority and requester identity.
- Any relevant service status or troubleshooting steps already attempted.

The assistant should explain why a ticket is recommended and summarize what information will be recorded before asking for confirmation.
